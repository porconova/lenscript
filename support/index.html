Support

Questions, ideas or something not working? Write to us — we read every message.

Contact us

Email: info@porconova.com

The easiest way is Settings → Support → Contact a human in the app: it opens an email with one line already filled in: the app version, your device and iOS version, Free or Pro, the app language and region, and your text size. Please keep that line. Nothing about your scripts or takes is attached.

If you write from elsewhere, please include:

  • your device (for example iPhone 16 or iPad Air) and iOS version,
  • the app version (at the bottom of Settings in the app),
  • what you did, what you expected and what happened — a screenshot helps a lot.

Reading and recording

  • Read or record. Open a script and tap Read to use the prompter only, or Record to read and record at the same time. The camera and microphone are asked for only when you tap Record.
  • Speed. Slide the right edge of the screen while the words move. 1.0× is about 150 words a minute.
  • Pause, go back, start over. Tap to pause, swipe the text to go back or skip ahead, two-finger tap to start over.
  • If the camera or microphone is not available. Another app or a call may be using it. Close it and tap record again. If you once said no to the camera or microphone, allow it in iOS Settings → Lenscript.
  • Free limits. Free takes are up to two minutes; in the last 15 seconds a countdown shows how much time is left. Free keeps three takes per script and three scripts of your own. Pro records without these limits.

Takes

Every take you keep stays with its script, newest first. Open a take to watch it, save it to Photos, share or delete it. To save every take to Photos automatically, turn on Also save to Photos in the app’s Settings. Takes are part of your device backup. Deleting a take in Lenscript does not delete the copy in Photos.

Purchases and subscriptions

  • Restore purchases. On a new device, sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Restore Purchases in the app’s Settings.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription. Open Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad, or go to your App Store subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.
  • Refunds. All payments are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Subscription or Lifetime? Lenscript Pro is a subscription, monthly or yearly, with three free days for new subscribers. Lenscript Pro · Lifetime is one payment for all of Pro, with no subscription.
  • If Pro ends. Nothing is deleted. Every script and take stays and can still be read, edited, played and shared. Recording continues with the free limits, and iCloud sync stops; takes already in your iCloud stay there.

iCloud sync

With Pro, turn on iCloud sync in the app’s Settings. Your scripts appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Takes sync separately with Sync takes, because video uses your iCloud storage — roughly 100 MB per minute. Everything is kept in your own iCloud; we have no access to it. If scripts do not appear on another device, check that you are signed in to iCloud, that iCloud Drive is turned on and that there is free iCloud storage.

Your data

Lenscript has no accounts and collects no data. Your scripts and takes stay on your device, or in your own iCloud if you turn sync on. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
