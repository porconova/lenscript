FAQ

Questions and answers

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. iCloud sync, if you turn it on, uses your own Apple Account.

Is recording really free?

Yes. Recording video with sound is free and never carries a watermark. Free takes are up to two minutes, with three takes per script, using the front camera and the built-in mic. Pro records without these limits.

Where do my takes go?

Every take you keep stays with its script in the app, newest first. Save it to Photos with a tap, or turn on saving to Photos automatically. Takes are part of your device backup.

How do I change the speed while I read?

Slide the right edge of the screen up or down. 1.0× is about 150 words a minute, and you can go from 0.5× to 2.0×.

Can I read without recording?

Yes. Open a script and tap Read. The camera and microphone are asked for only when you tap Record, and reading works without them.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. On iPhone the prompter is in portrait; on iPad it turns with the device.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Reading and recording work in airplane mode. Purchases need the App Store, and iCloud sync needs a connection.

Which files can I import?

With Pro: PDF, Word, RTF and plain text files, or text shared from any app. The text is read on your device and the file is not uploaded anywhere.

Does it work with a glass teleprompter rig?

Yes. Turn on Mirror and the text is flipped for the glass.

Can I use a remote or a keyboard?

With Pro, a Bluetooth remote or a keyboard controls the prompter while you read or record: Space pauses, ← → move through lines, ↑ ↓ change the speed.

What happens if Pro ends?

Nothing is deleted. Every script and take stays and can still be read, edited, played and shared. Recording continues with the free limits, and iCloud sync stops.

I got a new iPhone or iPad. How do I get Pro back?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Restore Purchases in the app's Settings. Turn on iCloud sync to bring your scripts and takes along.
