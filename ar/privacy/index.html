Lenscript

سياسة الخصوصية

آخر تحديث: November 24, 2026

هذا المستند متاح باللغة الإنجليزية. والنسخة الإنجليزية هي النسخة المعتمدة.

The short version: Lenscript does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your data. Your scripts stay on your device and — only if you turn it on — in your own private iCloud. Takes you record stay on your device, inside the app, and go to your Photos library only if you ask. Nothing is ever sent to the developer.

Developer

Lenscript is developed by Dzianis Milasheuski, an individual developer based in Lithuania. Questions about this policy: info@porconova.com

What I collect

Nothing. Lenscript has no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, and no servers that receive your data. There is no sign-up and no login. There are no third-party SDKs and no trackers in the app. The app makes no network requests of its own. I do not know who you are, what you read, or what you record.

The one exception is not mine to control and never contains your content: if you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics and crash reports with app developers, Apple may show me aggregated, anonymised statistics — how often the app crashed, on which iOS versions. This comes from Apple, in aggregate, and cannot be traced back to you or to anything you have written or recorded. You can turn it off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

What the app holds

  • Your scripts: the titles and text you type or paste, and where you stopped reading. They are kept in a local database inside the app’s private container on your device. The app also adds two built-in example scripts that explain how it works; you can edit or delete them.
  • Your takes: the recordings you choose to keep, with a short description of each (date, length, which script, how far you read). See “Recording” below.
  • Your settings: text size, speed, countdown, mirror, text backdrop, which camera and microphone to record with, and similar preferences. The answers you give to the short questions when you first open the app are used only to set these defaults. All of it is kept on your device and is not synced.

None of it is indexed, counted, analysed, or shared.

The clipboard is read only when you tap the system Paste button: iOS hands the text to the app at that moment. Before that, the app only checks whether the clipboard holds any text at all, so that it can show the button as available; it does not see what the text is.

Importing scripts

You can bring in a script from a file you pick in the Files picker (PDF, Word, RTF or plain text) or from the Share menu of another app. The text is read on your device; the file is not uploaded anywhere, and the app keeps only the text, as a new script. Only the file or text you choose is read, and only at that moment. Text shared from another app is placed in a folder on your device that only Lenscript and its Share extension can open, and becomes a script the next time you open the app. Without Pro it waits on the Home screen until you get Pro or remove it; a removed item is deleted. To tell you which of the two will happen, the Share extension can see one thing besides the text you share: whether Pro is active, a yes/no flag kept in that same on-device folder.

Recording video

Lenscript can record you while the words scroll — video with sound, video only, or sound only — with your device’s own camera and microphone, or with an external camera or microphone you connect. Recording is optional and happens only when you press the record button.

  • While a take is being recorded and reviewed, the video is kept as a temporary file inside the app’s private container.
  • If you choose “Keep”, the take is moved into the app’s private container on your device and listed under its script. If you choose “Retake”, close the take without keeping it, or leave the app, the temporary file is deleted. Leftover files are also deleted the next time the app starts.
  • Kept takes are included in your device backup (iCloud Backup or your computer), like other app data, and are deleted when you delete the take, its script, or the app. If you delete a take or a script on another device while “Sync takes” is off, its video file on this device is kept aside for 30 days, so it can be restored if the deletion was a sync mistake, and then removed.
  • A copy goes to your own Photos library only when you tap “To Photos” on a take, or if you turn on “Also save to Photos” in Settings. You can also share a take yourself with the system share sheet.
  • Lenscript never uploads a video anywhere except, if you turn on “Sync takes”, to your own iCloud (see below). What happens to a copy in your Photos library, or one you shared — including whether your Photos are synced to iCloud — is governed by your Photos and iCloud settings, not by this app.

PRIVATE iCLOUD SYNC (OPTIONAL, OFF BY DEFAULT, PART OF PRO) If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, Lenscript stores a copy of your scripts, and a short description of each take (date, length, which script), in the PRIVATE database of your own iCloud account, so your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account show the same scripts. Settings are not synced. Take files (video and sound) are synced only if you also turn on “Sync takes”. They are then stored in Lenscript’s private folder in your own iCloud Drive and count toward your iCloud storage. Turning it off keeps takes already in iCloud there; new takes stay on the device.

  • The data goes only to your iCloud. It does not pass through any server of mine, and I have no access to it.
  • Apple stores it under your Apple Account, governed by Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Turning sync off stops the copying; your scripts stay on the device.

Sync also uses Apple’s silent push notifications to learn that another device has changed something. These carry no content of yours; they only wake the app.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Camera — only in recording mode with video, to show you in the frame and record a take when you press record.
  • Microphone — only in recording mode with sound. The free version records with the built-in microphone; with Pro you can pick another one (wired, USB or Bluetooth). The choice is kept on your device.
  • Photos (add only) — only when you ask to add a take to your library. Lenscript asks for “add only” access: it cannot see, read, or change anything already in your Photos library.

You can use the prompter to read without granting any of these. Lenscript does not ask to send you notifications, and has no server that could send you anything.

What leaves your device

Only what you explicitly send yourself:

  • takes you choose to add to your own Photos library or share;
  • your scripts and take descriptions, to your own iCloud, if you turn sync on;
  • take files, to your own iCloud Drive, if you also turn on “Sync takes”;
  • an email you choose to write to support. The draft includes one line with the app version, device model, iOS version, whether Pro is active, the app’s language and region, the text size you use, and a random reference number that identifies nothing about you. You can delete that line before sending.

Nothing is uploaded automatically, and nothing is ever sent to me.

If the app cannot open its data

If the file that holds your scripts is ever damaged, Lenscript keeps the damaged file on your device, next to a new empty one, so that it can be recovered. It is not sent anywhere. If you write to support about it, I will explain how you can share it with me yourself — only if you choose to.

Purchases

Lenscript Pro is sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase. Apple, not the developer, processes payment. I receive no personal or payment information. You can manage or cancel a subscription in Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on your device.

Your rights

Because the app collects no personal information on the developer’s side, there is no developer-held data for you to access, correct, export, or delete.

  • Any script can be deleted inside the app.
  • Any take can be deleted inside the app. Copies you added to Photos are deleted there.
  • Scripts and takes in your iCloud can be removed by deleting them in the app while sync is on, by signing out of iCloud, or from your iCloud storage in iOS Settings.
  • Deleting the app removes the on-device copy.
  • An active subscription can be cancelled in your Apple Account subscriptions.

Information about other people

If other people appear or can be heard in a video you record, that video is kept only on your device and wherever you choose to put it. It is never transmitted to the developer or to any third party. You are responsible for recording other people only where you are allowed to.

Children

Lenscript is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

Regulatory notes

The app is designed so that no directly identifying personal information (name, email, IP address, advertising identifier, account ID) is collected by the developer or by any third party the developer uses. There are no third-party SDKs, no trackers, and no data brokers. Where Apple acts as a processor for purchases and for iCloud, those activities are governed by Apple’s own privacy policy. Because no personal data is collected or processed by the developer, there is no data controller obligation to transfer data internationally, and no data retention period to declare on the developer’s side. What you write and record is created, stored and erased entirely by you; the developer is neither its controller nor its processor and cannot reach it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions or concerns: info@porconova.com
